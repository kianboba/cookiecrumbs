How it works

Three steps, zero configuration. It starts protecting the moment it's installed.

  1. 1

    Browse normally

    Cookies are set as they always are — you don't change a thing.

  2. 2

    CookieCrumbs watches locally

    Every cookie is detected and classified by an on-device model: Strict, Performance, Functionality, Advertising, or Unknown.

  3. 3

    Score & automatic protection

    Each site gets a 0–100 privacy score with a plain-English label — while tracking cookies and tracker domains are blocked automatically.

Everything in one panel

Detection, protection, and control — without visiting a dashboard.

Cookie Detection

Sees every cookie a site sets: first-party, third-party, session, persistent, JavaScript-set, and partitioned.

Classification & Cookie Table

Every cookie sorted into five purpose categories, listed with party, lifespan, and security flags. Click a row for plain-English detail.

Privacy Score

A 0–100 score for every site, based on cookie category, party, persistence, and known-tracker status — with an honest label, not a vibe.

Tracking Protection

Blocks tracking cookies as you browse and stops tracker domains at the network level before their requests fire. Essential cookies are preserved. Always on, by design.

Session Guard

Removes weak session cookies — the ones missing Secure or HttpOnly — after you leave a site, so they can't be hijacked later. Toggleable anytime.

Consent Transparency

Before you accept a cookie banner, CookieCrumbs pauses and shows what will be collected and which companies receive it.

What CookieCrumbs never does

A tool about cookies shouldn't come with cookies of its own. CookieCrumbs is local-first: every analysis happens on your machine, inside your browser.

  • Doesn't collect, transmit, or sell any data
  • Has no servers — there's nothing to log in to
  • No accounts, no telemetry, no analytics
  • Sets no cookies of its own

The source of truth is your own browser: disconnect any network and everything keeps working.

FAQ

Does CookieCrumbs collect my data?

No. Every analysis runs inside your browser. Nothing is transmitted anywhere — the extension even works fully offline. Each Chrome permission exists for a local job: reading cookies, blocking tracker requests, and rendering the side panel.

Will it break websites?

Both protection systems come with per-site exemptions. If a site misbehaves, click Exempt this site from the panel — two independent whitelists exist: one for Tracking Protection, one for Session Guard.

Can I turn off blocking?

Tracking Protection is always on by design — that's the point. Session Guard is individually toggleable, and per-site exemptions are available for both.

What powers the classification?

An on-device model trained on public research datasets: CookieBlock (USENIX 2022), the Open Cookie Database, Disconnect.me tracker lists, and Consent-O-Matic category data. The datasets link in the trust strip above are the same sources.

Why “Load unpacked”? Will it be on the Web Store?

A Web Store listing is planned; until then, manual install is the official method. A store badge slot is reserved on this page.

Which browsers are supported?

Chrome and any Chromium-based browser with Manifest V3 support — including Edge, Brave, and Opera.
